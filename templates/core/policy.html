Eeshto Games Library Policy

Eeshto presents the Games Library that offers a variety of games for students, staff and faculty to use.

Basic Terms

  • You may borrow any game available by filling the EGL Booking Sheet beforehand.
  • While collecting the game, you will have to pay a security deposit as listed on the EGL Booking Sheet.
  • You can only borrow games during our set slots twice a week.
  • You may borrow a maximum of 2 games at a time.
  • If you want to borrow the game for a whole week please sign up for both of that week's slots.
  • The games must be returned to the assigned Eeshto member during normal operation hours during the next slot.
  • Upon return, you will be refunded the security deposit.
  • Late fees are INR 50.00 per every slot missed.
  • Once issued, you are responsible for the game. If you lose/damage the game you will be charged for repairs or replacement (see terms below).

Borrowing Process

  1. Sign up on the EGL Booking Sheet.
  2. Come to the Student Commons on the 2nd floor of the mess at the mentioned time (the second half hour of the slot).
  3. We are located near the couches between the meeting rooms.
  4. Pay the security deposit for the game, we only accept UPI payments.
  5. Games can be rented on a first-come, first-served basis if there aren't any prior reservations made for the aforementioned game. (Note: Rentals of such kind will only be entertained if and when there are no reservations made.)

Return Process

  • Please bring the game to the student commons on the 2nd floor of the mess at the mentioned time (the first half hour of the slot preferably).
  • Exceptions may be arranged by emailing Eeshto, or the HOD (spriha.chakraborty_ug2023@ashoka.edu.in).
  • The game will be assessed by the assigned Eeshto member, and if approved, it will be checked in and the security deposit will be returned.
  • In case it is not approved, see Terms for Damaged or Missing Components and Terms for Lost Games.

Hours of Operation

We have 2 slots per week, usually on Tuesday and Friday but may vary. The dates and timings are listed in the booking sheet, as well as our weekly emails.

Terms for Damage or Missing Components

  • Borrowers must inspect the game they are checking out before leaving.
  • Upon checkout, the borrower assumes full responsibility for the game(s) and all components.
  • All returned game(s) will be assessed for condition and components.
  • In case of any missing pieces or damage to the box/other components of the game, a replacement fee along with a penalty will be charged to the most recent borrower, up to the cost of the game.

Terms for Lost Games

  • If the game is not returned 30 days after its due date, it will be considered lost and the borrower will then be charged the full cost of replacing the game.
  • The borrower will be sent an invoice for replacement and a late fee will be charged.
  • This must be paid in full (no partial payments allowed) unless the game is being returned or replaced, in which case only the late fee would need to be paid.
  • Replaced games must be the same or later edition.
  • Eeshto may impose a temporary ban on borrowers who have lost games, repeated offenders may be permanently blacklisted from using EGL.

Terms for RAs, Clubs and Societies

Clubs and Societies

  • A club or society may choose to borrow independently for their events. In this case, all terms for an individual will continue to apply.
  • A security deposit will be collected at the time of borrowing and will be refunded upon return and inspection of the games.
  • In the event that the club/society has previously damaged or lost a game, Eeshto will charge a higher security deposit (MRP of the cost for a replacement).
  • Upon 2 incidents of damage or loss, the club/society will be banned from EGL for the next 2 semesters.

Resident Assistants

  • Resident Assistants may borrow up to 4 games for floor events without paying the security deposit, however please send an email ahead of time to check availability of the games.
  • All other terms still apply.

Terms for Eeshto Members

  • An assigned Eeshto member is expected to accurately document the borrower's relevant information and document the check in/out of games.
  • An assigned Eeshto member is expected to examine all the games that are borrowed and returned, document their condition and keep track of all games.
  • If damaged or missing pieces are found after the assigned member has checked in the game, then the member will be held responsible.
  • The assigned member must only edit the library documents from their personal Ashoka email ID.
  • Eeshto members may borrow games by the same procedure.
