Eeshto Games Library Policy

Eeshto presents the Games Library that offers a variety of games for students, staff and faculty to use. Our aim is to make gaming accessible to everyone at Ashoka.

Basic Terms

  • Games are available for checkout to all students, staff, and faculty. Eeshto will try its best to work with you; the harsher conditions below are reserved for the most extreme situations.
  • There are 2 types of EGL slots: Borrowing and Returning slots. Each happens once a week. You borrow games during the borrowing slot and return them during the return slot.
  • You may borrow any available game by filling the EGL Booking Sheet beforehand. While collecting the game, you pay the security deposit for its category. Games may only be borrowed during the set borrowing slots.
  • A student may borrow a maximum of 2 games at a time and return them by the next return slot. The same game cannot be borrowed consecutively, to keep things fair for everyone.
  • Games must be returned to the assigned Eeshto member during normal operation hours in the return slot. After a successful return, the security deposit is refunded within one week.
  • Late fees are INR 25.00 per game per slot missed. For example, a borrower with 2 games who misses 2 return slots owes (25 x 2) x 2 = INR 100.
  • Once issued, you are responsible for the game. If you lose or damage it you will be charged for repairs or replacement (see below).
  • Eeshto may impose a 1 semester probation, followed by a temporary ban of 1 semester, on borrowers who have lost, destroyed or mishandled games. Repeat offenders may be permanently blacklisted.
  • If a borrower has unresolved dues with Eeshto, they may be barred from borrowing until those dues are resolved. If a borrower is not communicating with Eeshto in any capacity despite multiple attempts to reach them, EGL may escalate the matter to the Committee Against Disciplinary Infractions (CADI). Eeshto will try its best to resolve infractions without a formal escalation.
  • A borrower who is graduating in the current semester needs a non-graduating student to vouch for them after the midterm break. We require an email from the vouching student. From then on the game is treated as borrowed by the vouching student only.
  • A borrower on probation is under heightened scrutiny. They are one step away from being barred for a semester, and any further infraction during probation results in that bar.
  • A borrower who has been put on probation, banned for 1 semester, and then put on probation again will receive a permanent ban for their next ban.
  • No game may be taken outside the premises of the University under any circumstances. Violating this may result in a fine and/or probation. Students living in TDI or pre-approved alternative housing are exempt.

Borrowing Process

  1. Sign up on the EGL Booking Sheet, entering your name, phone number and email address.
  2. Come to the Student Commons on the 2nd floor of the mess during the borrow slot. We are near the couches between the meeting rooms. Email eeshto@ashoka.edu.in with any questions about location or timing.
  3. Pay the security deposit for the game. We can only accept UPI payments.
  4. Games can be borrowed first come, first served if there are no prior reservations for that game. Such borrows are only entertained when there are no reservations, at the discretion of the assigned Eeshto member.
  5. Check the Inventory for any games you are borrowing. The Inventory is attached to the booking sheet.

Return Process

  • Bring the game to the Student Commons on the 2nd floor of the mess during the return slot (same place as borrowing). Rarely, exceptions may be arranged by emailing Eeshto or the HOD (varenya.garg_ug2025@ashoka.edu.in).
  • The game is assessed by the assigned Eeshto member. If approved it is checked in and the security deposit is returned within a week.
  • If it is not approved, see Terms for Damaged or Missing Components and Terms for Lost Games.

Hours of Operation

There is one borrow slot and one return slot each week. For now the return slot is each Tuesday, 8:30 PM to 9:30 PM, and the borrow slot is each Wednesday, 8:30 PM to 9:30 PM. Timings may vary, so check your mail to confirm. The dates and timings are listed on this site, in the booking sheet and in our emails.

Terms for Damage or Missing Components

  • Inspect the game before leaving, or within 3 hours of borrowing. After 3 hours the borrower assumes full responsibility for the game and all components. If you find damage you did not cause, report it on My Dues or email eeshto@ashoka.edu.in within 3 hours of borrowing.
  • All returned games are assessed for condition and components.
  • For missing pieces or damage to the box or other components, a replacement fee is charged to the offending borrower. It depends on severity and ranges from INR 50 to 100% of the MRP, and may rarely be waived at the society's discretion. The borrower is also put on probation for 1 semester.
  • If damage or lost components make the game unplayable beyond restoration, the borrower must pay 100% of the MRP or replace it with a new copy of the same or a later edition.
  • The replacement fee roughly follows the quantity and category of components affected: critical components are likely 100% or near 100% of the MRP, essential 50-75%, necessary 25-50%, and cosmetic around 25%. This scale is a guide and not a rubric. The final fee is the decision of the attending Eeshto member or the society.
  • Late fees are completely separate. They are charged on top of existing fees and have nothing to do with the replacement fee.

Categorisation of Game Components

  • Box/Containers: containers are treated as one of the other four categories depending on their nature.
  • Critical: integral to playability. If one is damaged or lost the game is unplayable (for example a card in Cluedo or the board in Shasn).
  • Essential: needed for the game to function properly. Without one, playability is permanently reduced but not lost (for example a die in Risk).
  • Necessary: part of the mechanics, but losing one or two does not reduce the game much (for example resource cards in Catan).
  • Cosmetic: do not contribute to the mechanics at all (for example the plastic houses in Game of Life).
  • As a group, components may fall in a higher category than they do individually. For example, one poker chip is necessary, but 25% of all chips is essential and 50% is critical. How groups are placed is left to the attending Eeshto member and the society.

Terms for Lost Games

  • If a game is not returned 3 return slots after its due date it is considered lost, and the borrower must either replace it or pay 100% of the MRP.
  • The borrower is sent a notice for replacement and late fees are charged. This must be paid in full (no partial payments) unless the game is being returned or replaced, in which case only the late fee is due. Replaced games must be the same or a later edition.
  • Late fees keep accruing at their normal rate between borrowing and return, even after 3 slots.
  • Contact Eeshto as soon as you lose a game and we will work with you toward the best reasonable outcome. Delaying only adds late fees.
  • In extremely rare cases replacement fees may be waived or reduced, solely at the society's discretion.

Terms for RAs, Clubs and Societies, Course TAs/TFs

Clubs and Societies

  • A club or society may borrow as a club, separately from normal student borrowing, by informing Eeshto at least 2 days in advance. Clubs may borrow up to 6 games with no other restriction.
  • The security deposit is the club/group deposit listed for each game on the booking sheet (generally 25% of the MRP up to INR 1000, or the game's category deposit, whichever is higher). It is collected at borrowing and refunded after return and inspection.
  • Eeshto needs a mail from the club email ID 2 days ahead with: the games being borrowed, a contact person, the club name, the purpose, and the date by which the games will be returned. On this site you give these details when requesting the reservation.
  • If a club or society damages or loses a game, the Terms for Damaged or Missing Components and Lost Games apply.
  • After one incident of damage or loss, the club's security deposit becomes 50% of the MRP and it is put on a 1 semester probation.
  • After 2 incidents, the club is banned from EGL for 1 semester, followed by a 1 semester probation.
  • Eeshto may refuse a club or society's request, especially after repeated, aggressive or inconsiderate behaviour or late responses.

Resident Assistants

  • RAs may borrow up to 4 games for floor events without paying the security deposit. Request at least 2 days ahead (more notice is appreciated). All other terms for students still apply, and RAs are still liable for late fees.
  • Provide: the games being borrowed, your name and phone number, your RH and floor, the purpose, and the date by which you will return them. Use your official Ashoka email ID.

Teaching Assistants / Fellows and others

  • Teaching Assistants and Fellows may borrow games for class events with no restriction on number. The concerned Professor must email Eeshto at least 2 days ahead with as many details as possible. All other terms still apply, and only games available to borrow can be lent.
  • Any person or group not covered here, such as independent student films, borrows under the basic student terms. Contact Eeshto or the HOD (varenya.garg_ug2025@ashoka.edu.in) if those terms cause difficulty; we may set ad-hoc terms that suit both sides.

Terms for Eeshto Members

  • An assigned Eeshto member accurately documents the borrower's relevant information and the check in/out of games.
  • An assigned member examines all games that are borrowed and returned, documents their condition and keeps track of all games.
  • The assigned member only edits library documents from their official Ashoka email ID.
  • Eeshto members may borrow games for personal use by the same procedure as a student, without a deposit.
  • Eeshto and its members may borrow games for Eeshto Society events without limits on duration or quantity.
  • The assigned member logs every transaction in the Transaction Log.
